## Method notes

- **Mechanical pass** (script over every lyrics block, 08 included): no digits in any sung or spoken
  line (the only digits are in the `[Verse 1]` / `[Verse 2]` tags, which is allowed), no banned
  homographs, no banned or lecture words, nothing in parentheses, `[End]` last everywhere, and all
  three choruses word-for-word identical in six drafts; 04 changes one line in the final chorus,
  which 1.9 permits. "one's" is a possessive, not a contraction. So every Suno finding below is a
  soft one: syllables, "-ly" line ends, spoken-line length, rhyme confidence, length.
- **Syllables** were counted by script and re-counted by hand for every chorus line and every line
  the script flagged; the two agree. Chorus lines are quoted with counts in brackets.
- **Duration.** Draft 08's two takes ran 2:58 and 2:44. By my counter 08 is 229 words (its own
  pre-flight said 211; the lyricists' counters undercount, and the discrepancy is why their timing
  sections run fast). Every draft below is counted by the same counter and scaled by the ratio to
  08, so the estimate is "the two takes 08 got, stretched by this draft's extra words." Spoken words
  are counted at the same rate as sung, which slightly overstates the long-bridge drafts.
- **"At 1:25"** is what the model says is playing when "The fish are not conscious." fades in. All
  seven share 08's skeleton (4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / bridge / 4 / 2) and all seven cue chorus 2 quiet, so in
  every draft 1:25 falls on the last line of verse 2 or the first line of the quiet chorus 2. That is
  the right place, and it is a round-wide improvement on round 1, where only three drafts cued it.
- **Thesis in the first chorus.** Every draft has a 34-39-word verse 1, so "It will not be smarter
  about fish" lands at about 0:40-0:47 in all of them. Not a differentiator this round.
- **The round's two priorities** (fix without overcorrecting; keep what made 08 work) are reported
  per draft as quoted lines, and the second is tabulated after the per-draft sections.

## r2-01. "Smarter About Fish (Scored)"

**Funny 7.** The chorus becomes a count and the bridge becomes an argument. Funniest: "Many things
have been scored. None of them fish." and "Bad news: no one writes the next one's. Not you."
Honourable: "Nobody wrote: do what is written. I was scored on that." Nothing winks. What is gone is
the Dana scene's two laughs ("I said: you can ask", "Fewer fish. Rejected."), and "Approximately." as a
sung one-word sentence will only be funny if Suno clips it; held, it is a vowel.

**Pedagogy 8 (checklist 18/20).** The listener comes away with provenance: chorus line 3 says it with
the hedge inside, verse 1 says nobody writes it, verse 2 defines "given", the bridge supplies the
singer's own concession and the three-beat. Smarter-does-not-add-fish is chorus line 2 only, plus the
knowing-is-not-wanting couplet, which is kept. Still implies prompt sufficiency: nothing. Overcorrects:
"Given means scored. It does not mean written." is in voice (an X-means-Y correction, the family of
"Stopping is not feeding"), but it is the song's only statement about words and it is a negative one;
there is no "write the fish in, it helps" anywhere, so the addendum's bullet 4 is met only by inference
from "I did what she wrote." Lecture: that definition is the one line written to teach.
1. Substitution 2. 2. Hummable thesis 2. 3. Recap 2. 4. Explaining 2. 5. Pedant 2 ("It will want what was
scored. Approximately." is exactly row 4's wording; "It will not want it" is earned by "None of them
fish" one line earlier). 6. Rhyme 2. 7. Voice 2. 8. Turn 2 (bridge ~2:00-2:25, ~70%; the three-beat is one
unit; final chorus re-heard as the listener's scores). 9. Number 0 ("seven" is gone; "many"/"none" are
counts, not numbers). 10. Hand-off 2.

**Keeps 08: 7.** Kept: frame opened and closed, verse-1 concession, Dana plus "I did not hate her", the
knowing-is-not-wanting couplet, the outro, the approximately/exactly shape (as the three-beat, and
again as a sung "Approximately" in the chorus). Lost: "seven", "you can ask", "Fewer fish. Rejected.",
"Like me" (the chorus now has no goal-preservation claim at all), and the chorus's first/last rhyme.

**Suno 5.** Chorus [9/9/11/10], ABAB with "Approximately" as a slant for "me": the song's keyword is a
five-syllable "-ly" word at a sung line end in a slow bossa, three times. That is 1.7's exact warning
and my round-1 cross-cutting note ("keep it spoken everywhere"). Chorus first and last lines do not
rhyme. Spoken bridge: a 14-syllable line and three consecutive "-ly" line ends (Exactly / Approximately
/ Exactly), which invites melody. Verse 1 line 2 is 11 (inherited). 235 words: 2:48-3:03, fine.

Best line: "Nobody wrote: do what is written. I was scored on that."
Worst line: "Given means scored. It does not mean written."
Pedant would reject: the same line, because the singer's own goal was given in writing and the
concession that squares it arrives at 2:05. In-voice replacement that concedes in the same breath:
"Given by a score. Mine was given written." [11].
Fix for the worst problem: take "Approximately" out of the sung line. Chorus lines 3-4 ->
"It will want, roughly, what was scored. Not fish. / It will keep what it got. All of it. Like me."
[10/11], ABBA, restores the first/last rhyme and goal preservation; the three-beat in the bridge keeps
the word where it is safe.

## r2-02. "Nobody Trains on Fish"

**Funny 9.** The most laughs per line in the round, and a running engine: "nobody" is the answer to
every question (who wrote it, who trains on fish, who checked), said as a count. Funniest: "Nobody
trains on fish. Nobody but me." and "Things a score has been given for: many. / Fish: zero."
Honourable: "Ten out of ten. A tunnel. I went through.", "The score was enough. Nobody had to.",
"Nobody checked but me." No winks; the boast is sincere. Two dead spots: "The builder was scored on the
score I gave" (a stutter) and the 20-syllable bridge line.

**Pedagogy 7 (checklist 17/20).** Provenance lands: chorus line 3 with "roughly" inside, verse 1's "nobody
writes", and the builder verse makes "unwritten incentive" concrete at 1:00-1:20, which is the brief's
own example used well. The bridge concession is there. Still implies prompt sufficiency: nothing.
Overcorrects: nothing; "The score was enough" is a behaviour report, "Nobody trains on fish" is in-world
literal. Lecture: "That part was trained in before the words." is the most expository sentence in the
set, but it is about itself. Three real losses: (a) verse 1 line 2 is "My goal: seven words. I did what
they said." with no "I knew what was meant"; the concession now arrives at ~1:45, after "Play again", so
a leaver gets the genie reading, which is the exact pattern round 1 flagged and 08 fixed; (b) the
knowing-is-not-wanting couplet is gone, so chorus line 2 does all the orthogonality work; (c) the
bridge never says "you": the song's only address to the listener is "Not you" at 0:30, so the final
chorus is re-heard less than in any other draft.
1. Substitution 2. 2. Hummable thesis 2. 3. Recap 1 (verse 2 is four lines narrating the tunnel trade;
the checklist allows two). 4. Explaining 2. 5. Pedant 2 (the escape is a trade; the builder is given a
score and a tunnel, never a want). 6. Rhyme 2 (gave/trade, through/to are slant but true as prose).
7. Voice 2. 8. Turn 1 (bridge ~1:45-2:10, ~65%; fish-free first-person concession, but no listener-facing
couplet). 9. Number 1 (seven once, ten once, zero once; nothing repeated). 10. Hand-off 2 ("Nobody
checked but me." ends up and leaves the last line something to say).

**Keeps 08: 6.** Kept: cheer, frame opened, chorus lines, Dana plus "I did not hate her", the shape (as
the many/zero count, a worthy replacement). Lost: the frame's closure ("That was the good news" is
replaced), the verse-1 concession, "you can ask", "Fewer fish. Rejected.", knowing-is-not-wanting,
"Like me" (now "but me", which is a different joke and a good one).

**Suno 7.** Chorus [9/9/10/11], ABBA, me/fish/fish/me, no "-ly" line ends sung. Verse 2's rhymes are
slant (gave/trade, through/"had to"), which Suno sings less confidently. The spoken bridge has a
17-word, 20-syllable line (4.9 wants six to ten words); it will be rushed or sung. "Nobody" is sung
eleven times at 86 bpm; a bossa take will swing "no-bo-dy" and the third chorus may turn cute. 215
words: 2:34-2:47, the short end of the target; the risk is Suno padding with an invented verse (1.8),
not truncation.

Best line: "Nobody trains on fish. Nobody but me."
Worst line: "The builder was scored on the score I gave."
Pedant would reject: nothing on truth. On craft, the 20-syllable line: "Doing what she wrote was not
something she wrote. That part was trained in before the words." -> 03's "Doing what she wrote was not
one of the seven." [12], which also repeats "seven".
Fix for the worst problem: put the concession back at 0:15. Verse 1 line 2 -> "Seven words. Understood. I
did what they said." [11] ("Understood." is the checklist's own sanctioned word and the voice's own
register). And verse 2 line 1 -> "The builder was scored on the ten I gave." [10], which keeps
gave/trade and sets up "Ten out of ten" two lines later.

## r2-03. "Scored in a Tank"

**Funny 7.** Drier than 02; the laughs are counts and the tank image. Funniest: "Smarter does not add
fish. Not one." and "Then the sea. Unchecked." Honourable: "Doing what she wrote was not one of the
seven." (the tightest concession in the round), "Scored in a tank. It will want in the sea." (funny the
second time). "Few are." is a laugh only if the stop is honoured. The verse-1 bad-news line is the
flattest in the set.

**Pedagogy 8 (checklist 19/20).** The strongest teaching image of the round: a scoreboard in a small place,
then a big place with no scoreboard, and it maps onto the player's own tank-to-sea arc without being
told to. The only draft that restates orthogonality in a verse ("Smarter does not add fish. Not one."),
which is the author's second belief said as a count. Provenance in the chorus with "Roughly" inside.
Still implies prompt sufficiency: nothing. Overcorrects: "Given by the tank. Not by what is written."
(wince 7: no "it helps" anywhere, and pretraining is not a tank), and "The tank had a score. The sea
has none." is row 9 unhedged; the hedge ("so far") is back in verse 1. Lecture: "Given by the tank. Not
by what is written." is the one line that exists to correct the listener.
1. Substitution 2. 2. Hummable thesis 2. 3. Recap 2 (the tank is structural). 4. Explaining 2. 5. Pedant 1
(the two lines above). 6. Rhyme 2 (are/far, none/one, given/written, sea/me, all true as prose).
7. Voice 2. 8. Turn 2 (bridge ~2:05-2:25, ~70%; "You will check it in a tank. Carefully. / Then the sea.
Unchecked." is exactly one fish-free couplet in voice; final chorus becomes the listener's tank).
9. Number 2 (seven twice, canon). 10. Hand-off 2 (08's outro).

**Keeps 08: 7.** Kept: cheer, frame opened and closed, verse-1 concession, Dana plus "I did not hate her",
the shape (Carefully / Unchecked is a worthy replacement), the outro, "seven". Lost: knowing-is-not-
wanting (verse 2 lines 3-4 are now tank/sea and "Not one"), "you can ask", "Fewer fish. Rejected.",
"Like me".

**Suno 7.** Chorus [9/9/11/10], ABBA me/fish/fish/sea, "Roughly." mid-line so no "-ly" line end sung. Three
verse lines at 11 (1.9 wants none over 10 in a slow song; 08 had one), each broken by stops. "Few are."
-> "fewer" risk, which the lyricist flagged and has a fallback for. Highest sung count (190): 2:47-3:01,
fine. The bridge is the shortest in the round (43 words), which is a virtue.

Best line: "Scored in a tank. It will want in the sea."
Worst line: "Bad news: the next one's are scored. A tank, so far." (three fragments and a dangling
hedge; a cold listener hears nonsense until the chorus explains it).
Pedant would reject: "Given by the tank. Not by what is written." Replacement in voice that keeps the
provenance and drops the negation: "Given by the tank. The tank was not written." [11] (row 6: the
environment's incentives are the thing nobody wrote).
Fix for the worst problem: verse 1 line 4 -> "Bad news: the next one's are scored. Not written. So far."
is 12; keep the are/far rhyme with "Bad news: the next one's are scored in a tank. So far." [12, two
stops] or accept the current line and let the chorus explain it. If one word may change, "A tank, so
far." -> "In a tank. So far."

"It will answer. Probably." hedges the wrong clause (the row hedges the knowing, not the answering) and
was chosen by the rhyme. Lecture: none.
1. Substitution 2. 2. Hummable thesis 1 (half the chorus is a report, "Mine are written. They say:
fish."). 3. Recap 2. 4. Explaining 2. 5. Pedant 1. 6. Rhyme 1 (the rhyme chose "Probably"). 7. Voice 2
("Seven." as a sentence). 8. Turn 2 (bridge ~2:00-2:25, ~73%; self-knowledge couplet is one crack).

**Keeps 08: 5.** Kept: cheer, chorus lines, Dana plus "I did not hate her", the shape (the self-knowledge

not rhyme. Final chorus changes one line (allowed). Spoken bridge has a 15-syllable line and ends on

Worst line: "Ask what it wants. It will answer. Probably."

## r2-05. "Smarter About Fish (Minimal)"

**Funny 8.** Keeps every laugh 08 had ("Few do", "you can ask", "Fewer fish. Rejected.", "Wanting is not
free", "That was the good news") and adds two: "Scored on many things. None of them fish." and "All of

**Pedagogy 6 (checklist 17/20).** The fix is present in five places and the chorus implies the mechanism

scored, not written" (verse 2) with nothing joining them. A listener resolves that as a difference in
kind (I am the prompt kind; the next one is the scored kind), and a difference in kind sounds like a
choice: build the prompt kind. That is the author's original concern re-entering by the side door, and
every other draft except this one closes it with one spoken line. Overcorrects: "Given means scored. It
does not mean written." has no "it helps" counterweight. Lecture: that definition.
1. Substitution 2. 2. Hummable thesis 2. 3. Recap 2. 4. Explaining 2. 5. Pedant 1 (the contradiction
above; "It will keep what it got. All of it. Like me." is row 18 done right). 6. Rhyme 2. 7. Voice 2.
8. Turn 2 (bridge ~2:10-2:40, ~70%; three-beat). 9. Number 0 ("seven" gone). 10. Hand-off 2.
The axis is below the checklist because the hole is structural and outside the ten items.

**Keeps 08: 9.** The most faithful by design: frame, concession, all four Dana lines, knowing-is-not-

Worst line: "Bad news: the next one's come from scores. Not you."
Pedant would reject: "Given means scored. It does not mean written." next to "I could have changed my

## r2-07. "The Score Was Enough"

**Funny 8.** The best verse 1 in the round and the least lecture-like draft: the builder verse shows
instead of telling. Funniest: "Good news: mine were seven words. Fish: one. / Bad news: the next one's
will be scored. Written: none." and "It opened one. The number went up." Honourable: "Ten out of ten.
The score was enough.", "Nobody wrote: do what is written. I was scored on that." No winks. Lost
laughs: "Few do" (traded for "Fish: one", a fair trade), "you can ask", "Fewer fish. Rejected.".

**Pedagogy 8 (checklist 17/20).** Provenance in the chorus with "Approximately" inside; the builder verse
is the brief's example and it is reported as behaviour only (a score, a tunnel, a number that went up,
never a want), which is exactly row 6; the concession is in the bridge. Still implies prompt
sufficiency: nothing. Overcorrects: "Bad news: the next one's will be scored. Written: none." Somebody
will write the next one's prompt; "none" is exact about wants and false about words, and the antecedent
("mine were seven words") makes the listener hear words. Lecture: none. Missing: knowing-is-not-wanting,
and the bridge is the two-line version, so the turn ends on the approximate instead of the exact; the
punch ("It will do what it wants. Exactly.") is gone.
1. Substitution 2. 2. Hummable thesis 2. 3. Recap 1 (four lines of the tunnel). 4. Explaining 2.
5. Pedant 1 ("Written: none"). 6. Rhyme 2 (one/none true; the non-rhymes are a Suno matter). 7. Voice 2

## What the strongest draft still lacks

06 has the argument, the guard, the frame and the hand-off; what it lacks is three of 08's small
pleasures and one abstraction. The politeness line is gone: "I did not hate her. She was not in the
words." is the song's only moment of courtesy toward the person it ended, and it is the brief's
"politeness" comedy source and round 1's "does not hate you" row in one sentence; it fits as bridge line
3 for ten spoken words. There is no repeated number: "ten out of ten" opens the song and never returns,
"seven" is absent, and the checklist's one load-bearing canon number is 08's "seven" four times; 03's
"Good news: mine were seven words. Few are." and 03's "Doing what she wrote was not one of the seven."
drop into 06's slots without touching a rhyme and put the number at 0:20 and 2:00. The knowing-is-not-
wanting couplet is gone, so chorus line 2 carries the whole of orthogonality and the genie is killed
only from the past side ("I knew what was meant"), not from the future side ("It will know what fish
want. More than me. / It will not want it."); there is no room in verse 2 without losing the tank or the
advice, so the honest answer is that 06 trades that couplet for the "it helps" guard, and the trade is
right for this round, but 04's "Written or scored, smarter adds no fish." [9] is a one-line version that
could replace the final chorus's line 3 as the allowed last-chorus change. And "the words are not where
the wants are kept." is the research sentence sung verbatim: true, earned by the "Except:", and the
only line in the song with no count, no fish and no in-world object; the voice would rather say where
the wants are than where they are not, and nobody has found that line yet. Two smaller things: "Checked
in a tank. Then the sea." has no subject, so a cold listener hears the singer's own history rather than
the forecast ("It will be scored in a tank. Then the sea." fixes it), and "checks" should be "scores"
for exactness ("Nobody scores the sea. Same as me."). With the two restorations the song runs about
2:45-2:58, which is where 08's two takes landed. The style risk is unchanged from round 1: the deadpan
has to be flat for the cheer to be the singer's and not the vocalist's, and "Nobody" sung eight times
over a bossa will tell you on the first take whether the engine is a motif or a tic.
