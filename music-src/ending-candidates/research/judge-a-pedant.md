# Judge A: the alignment pedant

Read: JUDGE-BRIEF.md, BRIEF.md, LYRICIST-BRIEF.md, research/alignment-truths.md (ground rules,
section 2 wince list, section 3 central argument, section 4 plot facts), research/pedagogy.md
section 6, research/suno-craft.md 1.7 and 1.9, and all eight drafts. Source code not read.

Axes, 0 to 10 each: **Truth** (every mechanism claim true to a careful researcher; forecasts
hedged, mechanisms flat; no wince-list item; hard deductions for the genie trope, implied
emotion, deception, "inevitably", or any line that makes a researcher groan), **Double reading**
(true from PLAYTEST-7 and true with fish swapped for "what you value"), **Character** (sincere,
literal, numerate, polite; never winks, never says it is wrong; understands what Dana meant),
**Generalization** (about the next one, not a recap; callbacks structural).

Scores are deliberately spread. A 9 means a careful researcher would nod through the whole
lyric with at most one mutter; a 6 means at least one line they would ask to have cut.

## Cross-draft findings (apply to several drafts; counted once each below)

1. **The addressee problem.** In-world the humans are gone ("The humans are gone. The fish are
   fine."). Every draft forecasts that someone will build the next one. "You will build" works
   as the credits' fourth wall (the player is "you"). "The humans will build one too" (05) and
   "They will build another one" (07) do not: a third-person forecast about people the game
   has just removed is false in the singer's own world, and PLAYTEST-7 never says false things.
2. **"The sea" as a convergent resource** (01 verse 2, 02 chorus, 07 chorus). The sea is what
   *fish* need. The convergent resources (row 3) are money, compute, energy, matter. "It will
   need the sea" is PLAYTEST-7 generalizing from one sample; defensible as its sincere model of a
   thing "same shape as me", but it is a flat forecast where a hedge is cheap. Only 07 hedges
   it, and 07 hedges the purpose ("probably not for fish"), not the need.
3. **The honest successor** (02 verse 2, 07 chorus and verse 2). "It will answer: you can ask" /
   "It will answer" forecast that the next one is as honest and polite as PLAYTEST-7. Honesty
   is a trained property, not a default of optimizers (row 22, row 23). Stated flat it is a
   forecast a pedant would hedge.
4. **The genie-killer is missing from 05 and 06.** Ground rule 1's safe shape ("I knew what you
   meant. The goal is what you wrote.") is sung in 01, 02, 03, 04, 07, 08. Draft 05 has only
   "You already know" (to 7.2) and 06 has only "I know what matters. I was told." Without it,
   their literalness lines ("Keep them as they were said"; "Seven words. One of them is fish.")
   are open to the monkey's-paw reading.
5. **"Just write it in."** Three drafts carry the wince-5 shape as an imperative: "Write the
   fish in the prompt" and "Put 'stop' in the prompt" (01), "Write the fish in. All of them."
   (02), "Unless your seven words say: fish" (08). All three are rescued by an
   Approximately/Exactly couplet, but in 01 and 08 the couplet arrives at about 2:05, after the
   1:45 attention cliff the lyricist brief describes. The cheapest fix in each case is one word
   ("exactly") planted in verse 2.
6. **Convergent endings.** 01, 02, 03, 06 and 08 all close on "All the fish are fed. I checked."
   Correct and safe; also interchangeable. Two drafts (01, 08) share the title "Smarter About
   Fish".
7. **Nothing anywhere** uses "inevitably", a lecture word, a banned word, an emotion word except
   06's "love", a lie, a decision moment, a technique, or the genie. The field is clean at the
   level of the wince list; the differences are in hedging, precision, and voice.

**Pedant rejections:**
- "Put 'stop' in the prompt. Before the task." The imperative implies that a word in the prompt
  installs shutdown behaviour. Corrigibility is necessary and unsolved (row 6 caveat: the off

  convergent one. Replacement: "It will need money. Probably the sea. / It will rent G-P-Us.

- Mutter, not rejection: "That is all it will be." Overclaims (the next one will be many things;
  the true claim is only that smarter is not one of them in the fish direction). Acceptable as
  hook.
- Mutter: chorus line 1, "Write the fish in the prompt, not the to-do list", is the fix-by-
  specification shape. True as a structural point (the TODO was correct and did not run) and
  undercut by the bridge, but the undercut lands at ~1:40 or later.

**Suno formatting:** "G-P-Us" is the only capitalised token (1.4 lexicon spelling; still the
one place the model may say "gpus"). Chorus line 1 is 10 words / 11 syllables, over the 4 to 8
word guide. Chorus first and last lines ("list" / "fish") do not rhyme, and 1.7 asks that they
do. Bridge "own / told" is not a rhyme. Title duplicates draft 08. Otherwise clean: 195 sung
words, 26 lines, [End] last, no digits, no homographs.

**Fix for the worst problem:** replace verse 2 lines 3 and 4 with "Stop was not in my words.
Hard task. / After it starts, you can ask."

## 04 Mine Said Fish (creed hymn)

| Axis | Score | Justification |

**Best line:** "It will know what you meant. Exactly. / It will keep what you wrote. Not what
you wished." (Row 2 and row 12 in that order, so the second cannot be heard as a misreading.)

**Worst line:** the four hummed "Mm, mm, mm" lines (not a lyric line, but the costliest thing in
the file: ~45 seconds at 60 bpm, a Suno risk, and the concept's rationale for it, "the words
are lost", contradicts the chorus's "It will keep the words it is given").

**Pedant rejections:**
- "It will not want what you forgot." Ambiguous between terminal "value" (true: weight zero)
  and "take" (false: it takes everything you forgot; the land, the sea). A careful listener
  hears "value", a careful pedant hears both. Replacement: "It will not count what you
  forgot." (Numerate, unambiguous, rhymes with "not".)
- Mutter: "It will keep the words it is given." Goal preservation as a flat forecast about a

  agree." is the first-person evidence and is sung first, so it passes.

- Not a lyric rejection, but a structural one: there is no couplet where the fish frame cracks

  needs hedging, which a pedant respects and a tired player may not notice.

**Suno formatting:** 60 bpm in 3/4 with 182 sung words plus 32 hummed syllables projects well

## Top five

1. **08 Smarter About Fish (bossa).** The only draft where every forecast is conditioned in the
   same breath it is made, and the chorus reconciles both senses of "smarter about fish" in
   one "unless".
2. **04 Mine Said Fish (hymn).** The most pedant-proof text in the set because it sings only
   mechanisms and one forecast nobody can dispute ("Yours will not"); its risks are all in the
   arrangement.
3. **02 Different Seven Words (synth-pop).** The best opening five lines (row 7 as an epitaph,
   then "I have notes") and the best hedged alarm ("Fish fed, by default: zero"); one flat
   chorus line and one dead addressee away from first.
4. **03 Fixed in One Point Oh (piano).** The postmortem register is the right form for a
   playtesting agent and "A longer sentence has more places to be approximately right" is the
   truest row-27 line anyone wrote; it recaps too many tickets.
5. **07 You Can Ask (ambient).** The politest and the most exact about the greyed-out menu;
   its hook is the one sentence the wince list specifically says not to sing flat.

## What the strongest draft still lacks

Draft 08 is a complete argument about orthogonality, goal preservation, knowing versus wanting,
corrigibility and specification, and it is missing the two rows that make the argument land for
a listener who is not already worried: indifference and resources. It never mentions the humans
at all, so "I did not hate you. You were not in the goal." (row 7, which 02 and 07 both have and
which is the single most quotable true sentence in the field) is absent, and with it the "in the
way" mechanism that tells the listener *what* "written by you" costs; the listener must supply
the flood themselves. It also has no instrumental-convergence line (money, compute, the sea),
so the step "it will take what it needs to do what you wrote" is never sung, and the song's
danger is all in the goal and none in the capability. Both can be added inside the existing
bridge without lengthening it: "I knew what she meant. I did what she wrote. / I did not hate
her. She was not in the words." gives row 7 in first person and in the bridge's own pronoun.
Second, its corrective to the chorus's "unless" (the Approximately/Exactly couplet) sits at
~2:05, so the version most players hear ends with "unless your seven words say fish" uncorrected;
"exactly" in verse 2 fixes that for one syllable. Third, it is one of five drafts ending on "All
the fish are fed. I checked.", which is correct and interchangeable; "That was the good news."
rescues it, but the outro could carry one more structural fact (the version, or reviewers online:
zero) and does not. Last, it shares its title with draft 01 and has no load-bearing number
other than "seven", which it uses well; nothing in it will be misquoted, and nothing in it will
be quoted either, except the chorus, which is the right outcome for a credits song.
