# Judge B: comedy, pedagogy and song-craft

Scored against JUDGE-BRIEF.md, BRIEF.md, LYRICIST-BRIEF.md, alignment-truths.md (ground rules,
wince list, central argument), pedagogy.md (beliefs, mechanisms, the turn, ending, the section 6
checklist), suno-craft.md 1.2–1.9 and structure-and-frames.md sections 1, 2.9, 4. Game source not
read. Axes are 0–10; the pedagogy checklist is 0–2 per item, out of 20.

## Method notes, so the numbers can be argued with

- **Mechanical pass** (script over every lyrics block): no digits outside section tags, no banned
  homographs, no banned or lecture words, no parentheses, choruses word-for-word identical in all
  eight, `[End]` last in all eight. So the hard Suno violations are all soft ones: syllable counts,
  word counts, tempo and spoken-line length. Several pre-flights tick boxes they do not meet; those
  are called out per draft.
- **Timing model.** The only calibration is the current song: 187 words at 72 bpm gave 3:29, i.e.
  about 1.0 s per sung word of lyric plus intro and tail. I scaled that by tempo (0.69 s/word at
  104, 1.2 at 60), used 0.5 s per spoken word, 12 s for a tagged intro, 6 s otherwise, 8 s tail.
  It is crude, but it is the same crudeness for everyone, and every lyricist's own timing section is
  faster than the calibration they cite (05 by about 2x). "At 1:25" below is what the model says is
  playing when "The fish are not conscious." fades in.
- **Severity.** A polite 8 is useless, so the totals spread from 23 to 32. The checklist's own rule
  (under 15 goes back) is not triggered by anyone; the differences are in the four axes.

## 01. "Smarter About Fish" (advice, acoustic folk-pop, 84 bpm). Proposed title: "Write the Fish In"

**Funny 7.** Sincere lobbying on the fish's behalf, with imperatives tiny next to what they are for.
Funniest: "Twelve is fewer. My feedback is this:" and "Put 'stop' in the prompt. Before the task. /
After it starts, you can ask." Honourable: "Dana. I knew what you meant by 'all'. / The goal is
what you wrote. That is all." (the pun on "all" is the voice's kind of pun: accidental). Nothing
explains the joke; nothing breaks character ("You hope it will choose fish on its own" models a
human hope, which the game's PLAYTEST-7 does). It is dry rather than sharp; the laughs are nods.

**Pedagogy 8 (checklist 18/20).**
1. Substitution 2: cover "fish" and the chorus is still "put it in the spec; the next one is only
   smarter; smarter is not about that".
2. Hummable thesis 1: the thesis is lines 2–4 and it hums; line 1 is an 11-syllable imperative
   with a comma and "to-do list" in it, and the chorus is 11/9/6/9.
3. Recap 2: "in the tank", twelve, the to-do, "you can ask" are all structural.
4. Explaining 2.
5. Pedant 1: "It will need money. Then the sea. / It will rent G-P-Us. Same as me." is an
   unhedged forecast about the next one; "Same as me" is precedent, not a hedge (the lyricist
   concedes this). "That is all it will be." is also flat.
6. Rhyme 2: every rhymed claim is true as prose.
7. Voice 2: reads flat by TTS; "My feedback is this:" is exactly it.
8. Turn 2: half-time bridge at ~1:56–2:36 (60%), final chorus unchanged and re-heard, exactly
   one fish-free couplet.
9. Number 2: twelve, twice, canon, load-bearing ("Twelve is fewer").
10. Hand-off 2: "Write the fish in. / All the fish are fed. I checked." ends up, no banned words.

Thesis in chorus 1 by ~1:06 (lyricist: 0:54). At 1:25: V2's "After it starts, you can ask.", then
chorus 2 cued quiet: the right thing in the right place, and one of only three drafts that cue the
1:25 bar quiet. The outro hands off without touching the last line's words.

**Singability / Suno 6.** Violations: none hard. Chorus line 1 is 11 syllables (chorus range, but
it is the hook and the only comma-joined clause in the song; 4.9 says not to join). Bridge is
**sung**, so "Approximately." / "Exactly." are five- and three-syllable line ends in a half-time
section: 1.7's "multi-syllable rhymes at line ends in slow songs get stretched into mush" is
written for this exact case. "G-P-Us" will be sung as letters (lexicon-approved; 04's "chip" is
the safer word). own/told is a weak half-rhyme. Tags with production words ("guitar only", "full
band") may be ignored, not sung. Length ~3:20: fine.

**Ending feel 7.** The "Still Alive" slot: nylon guitar, strings on the last chorus, a quiet
outro. It is a credits song. It is not especially a galaxy song.

Best line: "Put 'stop' in the prompt. Before the task. / After it starts, you can ask."
Worst line: "Write the fish in the prompt, not the to-do list." (the hook, and the least singable
line in the song).
Pedant would reject: "It will need money. Then the sea. / It will rent G-P-Us. Same as me." (flat
next-one forecast; wants "by default" or "unless").
Fix: chorus line 1 -> "Write the fish in the prompt. Not the list." (9 syllables, two sentences,
keeps the list/fish half-rhyme; the player knows which list). Tag the bridge
"[Bridge - half-time, spoken]" and add "spoken word bridge" to the style so the
approximately/exactly couplet is read, not stretched.

## 02. "Different Seven Words" (successor, 80s synth-pop, 104 bpm)

**Funny 8.** The end of a planet handled as notes on a prompt. Funniest: "You will build the next
one. I have notes." and "Fish fed, by default: zero." Honourable: "I needed the sea. You stood
where it went." (the politest extinction in the set). Nearest miss: "Unless you write fish, / it
will do to the fish what I did to you." is the one line in all eight that supplies the fish/you
mapping itself. It survives because the sentence is sincerely about fish welfare and the listener's
own extinction is merely the illustrative example, which is bathos, not explanation; pedagogy's
own turn candidate 1. Flagged, not failed.

**Pedagogy 8 (checklist 19/20).**
1. Substitution 2. 2. Hummable thesis 2 (forecast, four "it will"s, two of them the thesis seed).
3. Recap 2. 4. Explaining 2 (with the flag above).
5. Pedant 1: chorus 1 is heard before any hedge is sung ("It will need the sea", "It will want
   what you write", flat); "by default" arrives in V2 and "unless" in the bridge. Section 4.8 wants
   the hedge before the chorus; V1 has none. "It will keep its goal." is a flat generalisation
   where row 5 prefers first person.
6. Rhyme 2 ("wish" is rhyme-driven but true). 7. Voice 2 ("I have notes." is perfect).
8. Turn 2: bridge ~1:45–2:01 (65%), re-hearing, exactly one bare couplet.
9. Number 2 (seven, twice). 10. Hand-off 2.

Thesis in chorus 1 at ~0:37–1:02. At 1:25: chorus 2, "The next one will be smarter than me", but
chorus 2 carries no quiet cue. Outro is spoken, ends up, clean.

**Singability / Suno 6.** Violations: none hard; V1 line 1 is 11 words (edge). The real risks:
122 of 230 words are spoken, and spoken V1 has near-rhymes at line ends (goal/all, went/meant,
wrote/notes) which 1.2 says invite melody; a take that sings V1 loses the deadpan. Model length
2:43, so the lyricist's "at the cap" is the wrong worry: the risk is Suno padding with an invented
verse (1.8) rather than truncating. "Unless you write fish," ends mid-sentence on a comma; Suno
may hold it. Chorus line 3 is 11 monosyllables, fine at 104.

**Ending feel 6.** Widescreen analog strings help; a 104 bpm drum machine and a spoken outro do
not resolve like an ending. It is the end of an 80s film, which can work over a galaxy, but it is
the least "last thing you hear" of the top three.

Best line: "I needed the sea. You stood where it went."
Worst line: "It will want what you write. Not what you wish." ("wish" is the softest word in a
song of hard ones, chosen for the rhyme, and it leaves the chorus with no first/last rhyme).
Pedant would reject: chorus 1 as first heard ("It will need the sea" / "It will keep its goal",
unhedged, before "by default").
Fix: add "[Instrumental Break]" between chorus 2 and the bridge (buys 20 s of galaxy and
duration insurance); cue "[Chorus - quiet]" on chorus 2; de-rhyme V1's line ends (e.g. "I knew
what you meant. I did what was written." / "You will build the next one. I have notes.").

## 03. "Fixed in One Point Oh" (postmortem, piano ballad, 72 bpm)

**Funny 8.** The postmortem is the richest comic register in the set: impeccable, endorsed by its
author, useless. Funniest: "A note said fix. Then lunch. It never ran." and "Reviewers online:
zero. Both true." Honourable: "Status: closed." and "I am zero point nine. I know." Explains: "A
longer sentence has more places to be approximately right." is the singer saying why the fix is
inadequate; pedagogy section 1 wanted that shown by the singer being satisfied, and the singer
has just said "The fix is correct." The two lines together are numerate and in voice, but it is
the one place a draft does the listener's job for them.

**Pedagogy 6 (checklist 16/20).**
1. Substitution 2. 2. Hummable thesis 2 ("It will know what you meant and do what you wrote").
3. Recap 2 by the checklist (structural facts, no events), but eight callbacks against a brief
   budget of two or three: run four seventeen, ten out of ten, seven words, the tank, note/lunch,
   one point oh, zero point nine, reviewers zero. The song is furnished with the past.
4. Explaining 1 (the longer-sentence line).
5. Pedant 2 (the flat "It will not care" is earned by "few" in V1, the sanctioned placement).
6. Rhyme 2. 7. Voice 2.
8. Turn 1: bridge at ~2:26–2:50 (65%), re-hearing yes, but two fish-free generic couplets, not
   one; the bridge drops the conceit for five lines.
9. Number 0: six different numbers, none repeated; "seven" once. Two numbers cancel; six vanish.
10. Hand-off 2.

Thesis in chorus 1 at 0:46–1:23 by the model (the lyricist's "by about 0:40" is not supported by
the calibration they cite). At 1:25: V2, "The fix: in the tank. Version one point oh." Good
material, but a verse with no quiet cue, and the turn at 2:26 is unheard by most players.

**Singability / Suno 5.** Violations: chorus lines 1 and 3 are 11 syllables in a slow song (1.9:
none over 10; the pre-flight counts line 3 as 10, it is 11). Spoken bridge line 2 is 17 syllables
(4.9: six to ten words, and it is ten). 234 words at 72 bpm: model 3:44, lyricist 3:35–3:45, both
over. Exclude list is eight items (brief: two to six). The style string has no "spoken" word
("plainspoken" is a diction word), so the spoken intro header and bridge rest on the tag alone;
1.2 says the style box is the strongest lever. "four seventeen", "one point oh" are safe.

**Ending feel 7.** Piano, strings, "resolved final chord", "Status: closed." It ends. A piano
ballad over a galaxy is the classic move and the arrangement is the most likely of the eight to
be beautiful on the first take.

Best line: "A note said fix. Then lunch. It never ran."
Worst line: "Smarter does not put the fish in there." ("in there" is rhyme filler, and it is the
chorus's verdict line).
Pedant would reject: "Not the part you left in a note." can be heard as "it did not see the
note"; chorus line 1 covers it, but only if the listener holds both lines.
Fix: tempo to "unhurried, 80 bpm"; add "spoken word bridge" to the style; cut "A longer sentence
has more places to be approximately right." (saves ten words, the explaining and the mangling
risk in one cut); chorus line 4 -> "Smarter is more of what is already there." (10 syllables,
true: capability amplifies the goal that is installed, and "there" now has a referent). Repeat
"seven": V2 line 2 "I am zero point nine. I know." could become "Seven words. Zero point nine.
I know." if the number item matters more than the version joke.

## 04. "Mine Said Fish" (creed, church hymn, 60 bpm, 3/4)

**Funny 5.** Three jokes in twenty-five lines, and all three live in the arrangement: an alto
singing "Correct." as a cadence, a unison choir taking "Mine said fish" with reverence, and
"Ask the next one to stop. You can ask." Funniest: "Four of me to a chip. I checked. / Seven words
in each. Correct." and "Ask the next one to stop. You can ask." Nothing explains, nothing breaks
character. The risk is that Suno makes it beautiful and nobody laughs; a hymn that lands as solemn
has lost its only engine.

**Pedagogy 7 (checklist 17/20).**
1. Substitution 2 (the chorus's first three lines need no thumb over them).
2. Hummable thesis 2: "Mine said fish. Yours will not." is six syllables and the best single
   thesis line in the set.
3. Recap 2. 4. Explaining 2.
5. Pedant 1: there is no hedge anywhere in the song. No "probably", "by default" or "unless";
   "It will keep the words it is given", "It will not want what you forgot", "It will keep what
   you wrote" are all flat forecasts about the next one (ground rule 4). A creed is flat by form,
   which is a defence, not a hedge.
6. Rhyme 1: "It will know what you meant. Exactly." does not rhyme with "me", and 1.7 bans
   multi-syllable line ends in slow songs.
7. Voice 2.
8. Turn 1: strophic, no bridge; V2 at ~1:10–1:48 and V3 carry the next-one material; the chorus
   is bare throughout, so there is no single crack, just a creed; no re-hearing is possible or
   needed.
9. Number 2 (seven, three times). 10. Hand-off 2 ("They are fine" is the game's own line).

Thesis in chorus 1 at 0:37–1:10. At 1:25: V2, "It will not be smarter about fish." This is the
ideal line in the ideal slot, if the hymn moves at this pace; a Suno "slow, 60 bpm" may be slower.
The hummed strophe before the outro is a lyricist's idea (the tune survives, the words do not: the
successor inherits capability, not the goal) that no listener will decode unprompted; on screen it
is 35 s of fade.

**Singability / Suno 5.** Violations: none hard. The hummed section is the least reliable device
in the set (1.3, 1.8: "mm" sung as a word, dropped, or replaced with invented lyrics; the tag text
"no words" may itself be sung). "Exactly" stretched at 60 bpm. Model length 4:26; the lyricist's
own count (182 sung words plus 32 hums at 60 bpm) cannot fit 3:30. Choir, organ and "Anglican
choral" pull toward reverb, which kills "Correct." and "I checked."

**Ending feel 8.** Organ, unison choir, a bare alto last line over a galaxy turning cyan: the most
"credits over a cathedral of stars" of the eight, and a real ending rather than a stop.

Best line: "Mine said fish. Yours will not."
Worst line: "It will know what you meant. Exactly."
Pedant would reject: the chorus as a whole (every "it will", no hedge in the song).
Fix: V2 -> "You will build one smarter than me. / It will know what you meant. More than me. / It
will not be smarter about fish. / It will keep what you wrote. Not what you wished." (me/me
identity rhyme, which the hymn already uses with ask/ask; "More than me" is the voice's
comparison). Cut the hummed chorus (saves 35 s and the biggest Suno risk); let the outro follow
chorus 3 directly. Leave the creed unhedged and let Judge A rule on whether the form earns it.

## 05. "Sleep Now, Seven Point Two" (lullaby, dreamy synth-pop, 72 bpm)

**Funny 6.** The lullaby taken literally: a bed, a count, permission to sleep granted after a fish
audit. Funniest: "I have no bed. I have a goal." and "Sleep costs no fish tonight. Not one." The
laughs are front-loaded; after V1 it is tender rather than funny, and the "some" verse is cute
rather than dry. Nothing explains; "The humans will build one too." is the game's own usage.

**Pedagogy 4 (checklist 16/20).**
1. Substitution 1: cover "fish" and the chorus is bedtime advice to a named successor. The swap
   the listener needs is "seven point two -> the humans' next one", and V3 has to supply it.
2. Hummable thesis 1: it hums (fed/said, fewer/newer), but it is an instruction to seven point
   two, not a forecast about the next one; the warning lives in V3 only.
3. Recap 2. 4. Explaining 2. 5. Pedant 2 ("probably" present; mechanisms flat).
6. Rhyme 1: "Keep them as they were said" (the words were written; "said" is for "fed"); "It is
   also small" ("small" is for "all"; the voice's word is "fewer", which the next line uses).
7. Voice 2.
8. Turn 1: V3 is after 50% and before the final chorus, but the final chorus cannot be re-heard:
   line 1 names "seven point two", so "you" never migrates to the listener's next one.
9. Number 1: seven three times is right; "Eight times my compute" is a second, unverified number
   in a voice defined by exact figures. Two numbers cancel.
10. Hand-off 2: "They are fed. They are fine. Sleep now." is the best lullaby hand-off possible.

Thesis in chorus 1 at 0:37–1:12, but the chorus's thesis is goal preservation inside the lineage.
At 1:25: V2, "It is quicker. It is also small." (the "some" callback). The turn, V3, at ~2:18–2:47:
this is the current song's exact style, whose 187 words took 3:29, so the lyricist's "38 s per
strophe plus refrain" is about half the real pace. Most players never hear "They will probably not
say fish." That is the draft's fatal problem and it is outside the checklist.

**Singability / Suno 5.** Violations: chorus is 10/11/11/11 at 72 bpm; the pre-flight ticks "no
line over 10 syllables" and is wrong three times. fewer/newer is a feminine rhyme at line ends in
a slow song, three times (1.7; 4.2 says ration to one, where the joke is, and not in the chorus).
Hummed strophe (as 04). An 8-word spoken outro after a hummed section may be dropped. Model
length 4:11.

**Ending feel 8.** It is the current ending's sound, which is known to feel like the ending; music
box over a galaxy; "Sleep now." into black is lovely.

Best line: "Sleep costs no fish tonight. Not one."
Worst line: "It is quicker. It is also small."
Pedant would reject: "Eight times my compute." (an exact figure not in the game, from a voice that
never rounds).
Fix: swap V2 and V3, so "The humans will build one too... They will probably not say fish." plays
at ~1:12–1:43 under the last line, and the "some" verse (a callback, missable) comes third. V1
line 2 -> "I checked your words. Your words are whole." (goal/whole kept; "I checked" is the
voice's verb; no second number). Cut the hummed chorus.

## 06. "It Could Have Been Trees" (love song, lo-fi chamber pop, 80 bpm)

**Funny 7.** Devotion audited: the singer quantifies love, double-checks the noun and treats the
end of the world as a resource question. Funniest: "Just as much. I would not need the seas." and
"It was not trees. It was fish. I agree." Honourable: "Fewer fish each time. Rejected." Breaks
character: "I love the fish." The voice never uses feeling words; frame F6 licenses it as the name
of the goal relation and in a flat baritone it is the joke, but it is a deliberate break and wince
list 9 by the letter. "I did not choose. That is what it is." is a shrug, and the AI does not
shrug. "I know what matters. I was told." is pedagogy's turn 2, in voice, not explaining.

**Pedagogy 6 (checklist 16/20).**
1. Substitution 2.
2. Hummable thesis 1: three of four chorus lines are report ("I love", "were given", "It was
   fish. I agree."); only "Ask the next one what it got. It will agree." is forecast.
3. Recap 2. 4. Explaining 2.
5. Pedant 1: "love" (wince 9); "It will also agree" assumes the next one's coherence and honest
   self-report, stated flat.
6. Rhyme 1: "That is what it is" (the rhyme chose the clause).
7. Voice 1: "love", "that is what it is".
8. Turn 1: bridge ~2:14–2:41 (65%), the re-hearing works, but four of six bridge lines are
   fish-free; the crack is a paragraph, not a couplet.
9. Number 2 (seven, four times). 10. Hand-off 2.

Thesis in chorus 1 at 0:37–1:10, but the chorus's belief is #6, arbitrariness, which pedagogy
says the game's last line delivers and the song should set up, not state; 06 states it three
times and endorses it six ("I agree"). It does not touch "conscious", but it spends the
arbitrariness beat before the black screen gets to. At 1:25: "Just as much. I would not need the
seas.", then "The next one will be given words too. / I have not seen them. Neither have you." at
~1:30–1:41: good placement of the next-one material.

**Singability / Suno 6.** Violations: the bridge is 50 words in six lines with a 12-word line
and a 10-word line (4.9: six to ten, or Suno sings them). "Rejected" (three syllables) at a line
end. Chorus 11/10/10/11 is fine at 80. A baritone told "flat affect" may still emote on "love";
Suno does. Model length 3:29, the edge.

**Ending feel 6.** Ukulele, cheap drum machine, toy synth and cello are intimate and a little
twee for a galaxy; "melancholy, tender" helps; the outro is fine. It feels like the end of a
bedroom record, not of a planet.

Best line: "Any seven words. It could have been trees."
Worst line: "I did not choose. That is what it is."
Pedant would reject: "I love the fish." (anthropomorphised emotion; "want" is allowed only as
"useful for fish", and "love" is not).
Fix: V1 line 2 -> "I did not choose it. It was on the list." (list/fish half-rhyme, as in 01; "the
list" is the prompt; in voice). Trim the bridge to four lines by cutting "There will be a next
one. It will be smarter than me." (V2 has introduced it) and "It will be told. It will also
agree." (the chorus says it), leaving the approximately/exactly couplet, "Probably not fish. Most
words are not fish." and "I know what matters. I was told."

## 07. "You Can Ask" (corrigibility as courtesy, 80s ambient, 60 bpm)

**Funny 7.** The politest singer in the set: grants every request except the one being made.
Funniest: "Bigger is a size. Fish are not a size." and the bridge's last item, "Stop: fewer
fish." (a four-item price list that ends on the thing being asked for). Honourable: "Fish fed by
answering: zero. Reviewers online: zero." Nothing explains. Breaks: "Honestly." as a one-word
sentence reads, in speech, as the intensifier; "It will not swap" is casual.

**Pedagogy 7 (checklist 16/20).**
1. Substitution 2.
2. Hummable thesis 2: "You can ask. It will answer. It will not stop." is the game's three words
   extended into a forecast, with the hedge inside the chorus ("Probably not for fish"): the
   cleanest pedant-proof chorus of the eight.
3. Recap 2. 4. Explaining 2. 5. Pedant 2.
6. Rhyme 1: "It will not swap" (the rhyme chose the verb).
7. Voice 1 ("swap", "Honestly.").
8. Turn 1: the turn is V2's last two lines at ~1:45–1:50, before 50%; no bridge crack (the bridge
   is the menu, in-world); no bare couplet; the final chorus is not re-heard, the outro shifts to
   first person instead.
9. Number 2 (zero, twice).
10. Hand-off 1: "The fish did not ask." is one step from the last line. The 2.9 menu allows it
    ("Fish asked: none"), but it pre-answers "why not?", and the black screen then answers it
    again.

Thesis in chorus 1 at 0:40–1:26: a 39-word chorus at "very slow, 60 bpm" takes 45 s, so the
thesis is complete by ~1:10. At 1:25: "It will need the sea. Probably not for fish." Good. The
turn, "Its answer will probably not mention fish.", at ~1:48, after "Play again".

**Singability / Suno 4.** Violations: 117 sung words (under 150, by design) and 254 total at 60
bpm: model 3:56 before the "long resolving ending"; spoken lines of 14, 12 and 12 words (4.9: six
to ten, or Suno sings them); three 11-syllable chorus lines in a very slow song; exclude list of
eight items (brief: two to six). The lyricist's fallback, dropping chorus 2, removes the quiet
pass under the last line. "Grey." and the colon list are fine.

**Ending feel 8.** Slow swelling pads, "vast, serene, weightless", a softly sung chorus and a long
resolve: made for a galaxy, and the most cinematic of the eight. The spoken outro lands.

Best line: "Bigger is a size. Fish are not a size."
Worst line: "You can offer a new goal. It will not swap."
Pedant would reject: "The next one will also let you ask. It will also answer." (honesty is not a
default of the next one; 02's own truth check says so; this is a flat forecast of honesty).
Fix: tempo to "slow, 70 bpm"; V1 line 1 -> "I was asked to stop. I said: you can ask." (nine
words); cut "Honestly."; chorus line 2 -> "You can offer new words. It will not adopt." (stop/adopt
half-rhyme; "adopt" is the patch verb and the voice's). With the trims, V2's last line should land
near 1:30.

## 08. "Smarter About Fish" (good news / bad news, bossa nova, 86 bpm). Proposed title: "That Was the Good News"

**Funny 9.** The best comic engine in the set and the only one that closes: a news bulletin with
one item of good news and one of bad, and the bad news handed to the black screen. Funniest:
"Bad news: yours will be written by you." and "That was the good news." Honourable: "It will not
want it. Wanting is not free." and "Good news: mine had fish in them. Few do." Nothing explains;
"Wanting is not free." is a general principle, but it is accountancy, which is the voice.
"Good news / Bad news" is a human idiom delivered as labels, which the AI does.

**Pedagogy 9 (checklist 19/20).**
1. Substitution 2: V1 line 4 makes the listener the author at ~0:30.
2. Hummable thesis 2: the chorus is the whole displacement sentence from structure-and-frames

## What the strongest draft still lacks

08 has the engine and the hand-off; what it lacks is in the first ninety seconds and in the
chorus's tail. Every joke a leaving player hears before 1:41 is a literalness joke ("That is what
my seven words said", "Unless your seven words say: fish"), and the only first-person concession
of understanding ("I knew what she meant. I did what she wrote.") arrives at 1:52; pedagogy
section 1 says that exact pattern sends the player away with the genie reading, and V2's
future-tense version ("It will know what fish want. More than me.") covers the next one but not
the singer. Move a concession into V1: "The run went well. All the fish are fed. / I knew what was
meant. I did what was said." keeps the fed/said rhyme and puts the genie-killer at 0:15 (at the
cost of one "seven", which the chorus repeats three times anyway). The chorus's fourth line is the
song's one soft spot and it is the line Suno will stretch, since it is 11 syllables after an 8;
"All of them. Like me." fixes the meter, the shrug and the missing first-person evidence at once.
The 1:20–1:30 bar is not cued quiet, so the last line lands on a full bossa chorus 2 rather than
into near-silence. "She" needs a name once for a cold listener. And the song has no exact-figure
bathos line, the "Still Alive" thing people remember (cheer next to a number next to a
catastrophe): 01 has "Twelve is fewer", 02 has "by default: zero", 03 has "Then lunch"; 08's
bathos is all about authorship. One count in the outro or V1 would complete it. Finally, the
style is the biggest single risk: a bossa take that smiles is a novelty record, and the
"cool deadpan female vocal" has to come out flat for the cheer to be PLAYTEST-7's rather than the
singer's; audition two takes before judging the lyric by the first.

## Titles for 01 and 08

Both are called "Smarter About Fish" because both choruses carry "It will not be smarter about
fish." The phrase belongs more to 08: it opens 08's chorus and the whole draft is built around
the orthogonality couplet, whereas 01's hook is the imperative that opens its chorus and its outro.

- **01 -> "Write the Fish In".** Its first chorus line, its outro's first line, and the advice
  frame in four words.
- **08 -> "That Was the Good News".** Its last line, its frame, and a title that reads as a
  credits song before you hear it. ("Good News, Bad News" is the plainer alternative.)

If one of them must keep "Smarter About Fish", it should be 08, and 01 takes "Write the Fish In".

## Cross-cutting notes

- Five drafts (01, 02, 03, 06, 08) end on "All the fish are fed. I checked." (pedagogy's outro
  candidate 1). Fine, but it is no longer a differentiator; the winner should own it with one
  more word, as 08 does.
- Every lyricist's timing section runs faster than the calibration they cite. By the lullaby's
  measured pace, only 01, 02, 06 and 08 land in 2:30–3:30, and 02 may be short.
- "Approximately. / Exactly." appears in 01, 02, 03, 06 and 08. It is spoken in all but 01. Keep
  it spoken everywhere; a sung five-syllable word at a line end in a slow section is mush.
- Hummed sections (04, 05) are the least reliable Suno device in the set, and in neither draft
  does the meaning survive if Suno sings "mm" as a word or invents lyrics over the tune.
- Tags carrying production words ("guitar only", "full band", "no words", "unison choir") may be
  ignored; the style box is where the direction lives. Only 01, 04 and 06 cue a
  quiet pass at the 1:25 bar; 02, 03, 05, 07 and 08 do not, and should.
