## Cross-draft findings (counted once each; referenced below)

1. **The field is clean on the headline winces.** No draft sings "it will want the score",
   "secretly", "prompts are useless", "it will do what it was trained to do" as a certainty, or
   "nothing can be known" outright. The nearest approaches are 04's "Also: nothing to review."
   and 03's "Then the sea. Unchecked." The author's concern is fixed in all seven; the
   differences are hedging, counterweight, and what was lost from 08.
2. **The exclusion line.** "Given means scored. It does not mean written." (01, 05) and "Given
   by the tank. Not by what is written." (03) are the research's own suggested lines, and they
   are the one place the round leans past the addendum. The singer's goal was given in writing
   and the song has just said so; a flat "does not mean written" excludes the prompt from the
   pipeline, which row 1's caveat forbids ("never 'the prompt does nothing'; say 'the prompt
   is read; the reader was already made'"). Only 06 carries the counterweight the addendum asks
   for ("Write the fish in. It helps."). The cheapest repair keeps the rhyme: "Given means
   scored. Then it sees what is written."
3. **The antecedent of "the next one's".** In 01, 05 (and more faintly 03, 07), verse 1 line 2
   is "I did what my words said" and line 3 is "mine had fish in them", so "nobody writes the
   next one's" grammatically says nobody writes the next one's *words*, which is false (someone
   will write its prompt). 02 ("My goal: seven words"), 06 ("my wants were written down") and
   04 ("mine are written") give the pronoun a usable antecedent. A listener fills in "goal";
   a pedant notices the sentence. Fix in the drafts that have it: "Good news: my goal had fish
   in it. Few do."
4. **"None of them fish" / "Nobody trains on fish" / "Not fish" (all seven).** In-world these
   are literal (only the singer has trained anything on fish, and it checked its copies rather
   than scoring them). In the swap reading, preference training scores on something adjacent
   to what the listener values, so "none" is "zero for the whole, many for pieces", and that
   reading is carried only by "roughly" or "approximately" nearby. Every draft has the hedge
   within a line; none sings "something near fish" once, which the research offers as the
   stronger option. A mutter everywhere, not a rejection anywhere.
5. **The third beat.** "It will do what it wants. Exactly." (01, 05, 06) is a flat forecast of
   competent agency. It is licensed by the chorus's premise ("smarter than me") and "Exactly"
   lands on execution, not content. Accepted; noted once.
6. **What was lost from 08.** The author asked that the cheerful frame, the first two chorus
   lines, exact numbers, politeness, the Dana lines and the outro hand-off be kept. The table:

   | Kept from 08 | 01 | 02 | 03 | 04 | 05 | 06 | 07 |
