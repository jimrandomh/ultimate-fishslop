# Judge brief

You are scoring candidate ending songs in drafts/*.md. Read first:
- BRIEF.md (the game, the voice, the song's purpose)
- LYRICIST-BRIEF.md (the rules the lyricists were given, including Suno formatting)
- research/alignment-truths.md: ground rules (top), wince list (section 2), central argument (3)
- research/pedagogy.md section 6 (the 10-item checklist)
Do not read the game's source code.

Then read every draft in drafts/ and score each on the axes in your assignment (0–10 each,
with a one-line justification per axis). Also give, per draft: the single best line, the single
worst line, any line a pedant would reject (quote it and say why), any Suno formatting
violation (digits, banned homographs, missing [End], chorus mismatch, line too long), and a
concrete fix for the worst problem (one or two replacement lines, in voice).

Finish with a ranked list of all drafts, the top five with one sentence each on why, and one
paragraph on what the strongest draft is still missing. Write your report to the file named in
your assignment. Be severe; a polite 8 for everything is useless.
