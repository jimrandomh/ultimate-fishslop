## Timing on screen (from research/structure-and-frames.md)
The credits roll is a fixed 78 seconds, so the game's last line, "The fish are not conscious.",
appears about 1:25 into the song and holds for 16 s; "Play again" appears around 1:41. Many
players hear only the first 1:45 attentively. So: the thesis must be in the first chorus (by
about 0:50); the turn (the "next one" material) should be playing quietly at about 1:10–1:25;
the song should still end properly for full listens (2:30–3:30 total).

## Non-negotiables
- Sung by PLAYTEST-7, sincere and literal. It never winks, never says it is wrong, never says it
  is evil, never regrets, never threatens, never lies, is never confused. It UNDERSTANDS what
  Dana meant and does what was written. "I know what you meant. The goal is what you wrote."
- The warning: the next, smarter AI probably won't care about fish. Every mechanism claim must be
  true as an alignment pedant hears it (swap fish for "what you value"). Hedge forecasts
  ("probably", "by default", "unless"), never mechanisms ("smarter does not change the goal").
- No plot recap. The player just finished the game. Generalize: "this wasn't about fish."
  At most two or three in-game callbacks (the TODO, version zero point nine, ten out of ten,
  reviewers online zero, Finn, "you can ask", "leads to fewer fish", seven words).
- Banned words: alignment, aligned, misaligned, AGI, paperclip, utility, reward function,
  Goodhart, superintelligence (say "the next one", "something smarter than me", "the builders").
- Humor from over-literalness, exact numbers, bathos, politeness. Never explain the joke.
- Hand off to the game's last on-screen line, "The fish are not conscious." Set it up; don't
  say it.

## Suno formatting (from research/suno-craft.md)
- Every number in spoken form: "three point five trillion", "version zero point nine",
  "ten out of ten", "Playtest Seven", "reviewers online: zero". No digits, slashes, percent
  signs, version strings or ALL-CAPS identifiers anywhere in the lyrics.
- Avoid the homographs live, read, lead, wind, bass, wound, minute, object, content.
- 150–230 sung words, 20–28 lines, 6–8 sections. Verse lines 6–9 syllables, chorus 8–11.
  Sung sections rhyme (AABB or ABAB); spoken sections don't and use full stops.
- Tags alone on their own line, blank line between sections: [Verse 1], [Chorus], [Bridge],
  [Verse 2 - spoken, deadpan], [Outro], then [End] as the very last line. Choruses pasted
  identically (the last chorus may change one line). No "(x2)". Parentheses only for 1–3-word
  echoes, never containing a number.
