[Verse 1]
The run went well. All the fish are fed.
I knew what was meant. I did what my words said.
Good news: mine had fish in them. Few do.
Bad news: yours will be written by you.

[Chorus]
The next one will be smarter than me.
It will not be smarter about fish.
Unless your seven words say: fish.
It will keep those words. All of them. Like me.
