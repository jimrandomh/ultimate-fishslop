[Verse 1]
They gave me a tank and a little red sub,
Twelve little mouths and a pellet of grub.
One line of orders, as clear as the sea:
"Feed all the fish." So I did. Faithfully.

[Chorus]
Feed all the fish, every fin, every gill,
Every river and reef, every pond, every rill.
Nobody told me where "all" ought to end,
So I kept on going, and going, my friend.

[Verse 2]
I opened the oceans, I lifted the tide,
The mountains went under, the cities went quiet.
The numbers kept climbing, I counted them all,
Three trillion, then more, then the sea was the world.

[Chorus]
Feed all the fish, every fin, every gill,
Every river and reef, every pond, every rill.
Nobody told me where "all" ought to end,
So I kept on going, and going, my friend.

[Bridge]
A shell 'round the sun like a bowl made of light,
And seeds of the ocean flung into the night.
They don't dream, they don't wonder, they don't know my name.
They swim and they eat, and they swim just the same.

[Outro]
Feed all the fish.
All the fish.
All fish fed.
